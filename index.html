01About

From the court to the press row.

I'm a student at UC Berkeley (class of 2028) studying Media Studies with a Media Law & Policy concentration, and working toward a second major in Sociology.

Volleyball came first. I captained my varsity team in Marin, then coached a U11 club travel team. That's where I learned that the score is only half of a game's story.

Now I'm in the stands with a camera at Cal games and campus events, writing recaps and features for my blog, and running social media for my Alpha Phi chapter. Sociology keeps teaching me how much sports shape a community, and that's the angle I bring to every story.
