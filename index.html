About

I'm in UC Berkeley's class of 2028, studying Media Studies with a concentration in Media Law and Policy and working toward a second major in Sociology.

I grew up playing volleyball in Marin County and captained my varsity team. Coaching a U11 travel team afterward taught me how much happens around a game that never shows up in the box score, and that's what I try to write about.

At Berkeley I go to Cal games and campus events with a camera, write about them on this site, and run social media for my Alpha Phi chapter.
